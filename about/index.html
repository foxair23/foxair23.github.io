Our Story

Trusted Service Since 1981

Castle Garage Doors & Gates has served San Diego homeowners since 1981 with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable garage door and gate service.

Today, Castle remains committed to the same values that have defined the company since 1981: honest service, quality workmanship, clear communication, and treating every customer’s home with care. From our headquarters in Escondido, we proudly serve homeowners and businesses across San Diego and Riverside County.

We are licensed, bonded, and insured, a Clopay Authorized Dealer, and the exclusive Home Depot garage door service partner for San Diego County.

Our Values

  • Honesty — transparent pricing, no upselling, no scare tactics
  • Quality — we use premium parts and stand behind our work
  • Reliability — we show up when we say we will, every time
  • Safety — regular OSHA safety training for all technicians
  • Community — we live where we work and care about our neighbors
