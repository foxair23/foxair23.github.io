Common Signs You Need Garage Door Repair

Not sure if your garage door needs professional attention? Here are the most common warning signs:

  • Loud bang from the garage — this is usually a broken torsion spring, the most common repair we handle. Learn more about broken spring symptoms.
  • Door won’t close or reverses — often a safety sensor issue that’s straightforward to fix. See our troubleshooting guide.
  • Grinding, squeaking, or scraping noises — usually worn rollers or dry components that need lubrication.
  • Door is crooked or uneven — can indicate a broken spring, cable issue, or track misalignment.
  • Door feels extremely heavy — springs have failed and are no longer bearing the door’s weight.
  • Visible damage — dented panels, frayed cables, or gaps in spring coils.

Why DIY Garage Door Repairs Can Be Dangerous

We understand the temptation to fix things yourself, but garage doors involve components under extreme tension. Torsion springs store enough energy to cause serious injury or death if handled improperly. Cable drums, heavy panels, and high-tension hardware all require professional tools and training. Every year, homeowners are seriously hurt attempting garage door repairs that should be left to trained technicians.

Is it safe to open my garage door with a broken spring?
No. A door with a broken spring bears its full weight (150–250+ lbs) unsupported. Attempting to operate it manually or with the opener can cause injury or further damage. Call a professional for spring replacement.
