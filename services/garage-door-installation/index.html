FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new garage door cost in San Diego?
New garage doors range from $800 to $4,000+ installed for residential, depending on size, material, and insulation. A standard 16x7 steel insulated door typically runs $1,200–$2,200. See our full cost breakdown.
How long does garage door installation take?
A standard residential garage door installation takes 4–6 hours. Custom doors or installations requiring structural work may take longer. We handle removal of the old door as part of the service.
What garage door brand do you recommend?
As a Clopay Authorized Dealer, we recommend Clopay for their quality, variety, and comprehensive warranties. Their Imagine System lets you visualize doors on your actual home. Compare door materials.
Will a new garage door increase my home value?
Yes. Garage door replacement consistently ranks as one of the highest-ROI home improvements, often recovering 90–100% of cost at resale according to Remodeling Magazine's Cost vs. Value report.

Common Questions About Garage Door Installation

Does Castle install new garage doors?
