Why it’s worth doing now

The failure is coming either way

Springs are a wear part with a rated cycle life. Replace them on your schedule and it’s a planned visit. Wait for one to snap and it’s an emergency call, a door stuck shut, and a car you can’t get out.

You stop paying for hardware

Most homeowners buy springs again every seven to ten years. With Castle, if hardware we install fails through normal use, we replace the part free for as long as you own your home — so you pay for it once.

A balanced door is a safe door

An out-of-balance door strains the opener and can fall. We set the balance after the new hardware goes on, then verify the reversing sensors.

Lifetime Parts Protection — the details

Ask your technician to walk you through it.

01
Parts, not labor.

Castle supplies the replacement part at no charge. A labor charge applies once your labor warranty ends.

02
Original homeowner.

Coverage runs as long as you own the home where the work was done. It can be transferred to the next homeowner for an additional fee.

03
Annual maintenance.

A yearly Castle visit keeps coverage active. Premium includes the first three years.

04
Installed hardware.

Springs, cables, rollers, bearings, drums and the torsion tube — everything we install on your door.

05
Castle does the service.

All maintenance and repairs in between must be performed by Castle. Work by anyone else voids the warranty.

Not covered: garage door opener and motor, door panels and sections, cosmetic finishes, weather seals, damage from accident or impact, misuse, and modifications or repairs performed by others. Full terms are available at CastleGarage.com/warranty.

Questions? We’re happy to walk through it.
