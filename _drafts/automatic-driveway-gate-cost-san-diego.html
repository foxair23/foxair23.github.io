Automatic Driveway Gate Cost in San Diego

Automatic driveway gate pricing varies widely depending on the gate, the operator, access control features, and site conditions. Here's what drives cost — and what to think through before you call for a quote.

Gate Size and Material

Larger gates require heavier-duty operators and more material, which affects cost. Common gate materials include:

  • Wrought iron — classic, durable, commonly seen in San Diego
  • Aluminum — lighter, corrosion-resistant, good for coastal areas
  • Wood — natural look, higher maintenance
  • Steel — strong, often powder-coated for longevity
  • Custom fabricated — any design, higher cost but unique

Swing vs. Slide Gates

The gate type affects both the operator needed and the site requirements:

  • Swing gates require clearance behind the gate to open. Single-swing and dual-swing configurations each have different operator requirements.
  • Slide gates require a clear path along the fence line for the gate to travel. Often preferred on sloped driveways or tight entry points.

Gate Operator / Opener

The operator is the motor that automates the gate. Operator selection depends on gate weight, frequency of use, and power availability. Residential and commercial-grade operators vary significantly in price and durability.

Access Control, Keypads, and Intercoms

Access control options range from basic keypad entry to video intercoms, license plate readers, and smart-phone integrations. Each adds to the project cost but significantly improves convenience and security.

Electrical and Site Prep

Automatic gates require power. If your driveway entry doesn't have electrical service nearby, running conduit and wiring adds to the project cost. Concrete work, posts, and column installation are also factors depending on your property.

Custom Gates

Repair vs. New Installation

If you have an existing gate that's damaged, slow, or failing, repair may be significantly less expensive than full replacement. We assess each situation individually and give you an honest comparison.
