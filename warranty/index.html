Our Warranty

We’ve been putting our name on garage doors from San Diego to Riverside County since 1981. That’s a long time to be wrong about something in front of your neighbors, so we’ve never been interested in the kind of warranty that’s written to get us out of things.

Here’s how it actually works. If you hired Castle and paid Castle, we stand behind the work for a year — parts and labor, no arguing about which one failed. If you bought your door through Home Depot or another retailer and we installed it, the warranty is theirs, and we’ll point you to the right person. And if you had us do a Castle Restoration, the moving hardware we installed is covered for as long as you own the home.

Everything below is the detail. If you’d rather just call and ask, that works too.

Coverage starts on the date the work was completed. Your invoice is your warranty record — hang onto it, or call us and we’ll pull it up.

Work You Purchased Directly from Castle

One year, parts and labor. If you paid Castle directly for the work, and something we installed or repaired fails within a year, we fix it. No service call fee for a covered warranty visit. The one exception is wear items, which are spelled out below.

Repairs

  • Labor on all repairs: 1 year from the date of service.
  • Parts we supply and install: 1 year, against defects in materials or workmanship. Wear items are excluded — see below.

New Garage Door Installations

  • Door sections, panels, and factory finish: Covered by the manufacturer. Terms vary by model and run from roughly 10 years to lifetime. The exact coverage is noted on your paperwork, and we register it for you where registration is required.
  • Hardware and moving parts: 1 year from Castle.
  • Weather seals, bottom seals, and trim: Not warranted. See wear items below.
  • Our labor: 1 year.

Openers, Remotes, and Accessories

  • Openers (motor, belt or chain, logic board): Covered by the manufacturer. Most residential openers carry multi-year coverage on the motor and shorter coverage on electronics; the specifics are on your unit’s paperwork.
  • Our labor: 1 year.
  • Keypads, remotes, and smart accessories: Manufacturer warranty only.
  • We handle the claim. If a manufacturer warranty applies, you don’t have to chase it. Call us, we’ll file it.

Gates and Gate Operators

  • Gate operators: Manufacturer warranty on the unit, 1 year Castle labor.
  • Gate hardware we supply (hinges, rollers, tracks, chain, arms): 1 year parts and labor.
  • Welding and fabrication: 1 year on our labor.
  • Access control (keypads, intercoms, loop detectors, receivers): Manufacturer warranty; 1 year Castle labor.

Wear Items

Some parts on a garage door and gate are designed to be used up. They don’t fail — they wear out, on a schedule set by sun, weather, and how often the door runs. Because that’s expected rather than defective, wear items carry no warranty at any level, including on Castle Restoration.

  • Bottom weather seals
  • Perimeter weather seals and trim
  • Batteries in remotes and keypads
  • Light bulbs

One distinction worth drawing: a seal that’s dried out and cracked after four Southern California summers is wear. A seal that pulls loose two weeks after we installed it wasn’t installed right, and our labor is covered for a year like everything else we do.

Castle Restoration: Lifetime Parts Protection

Most garage doors don’t need replacing. They need the parts that actually wear out replaced — springs, cables, rollers, bearings, drums, and the torsion tube — and a door that’s been running rough for years comes back quiet and smooth for a fraction of the cost of a new one.

That’s the Castle Restoration. And because we replace the whole wear system at once instead of one piece at a time, we can do something we can’t do on a single repair: cover that hardware for as long as you own your home.

What’s Covered for Life

The moving hardware we install during your Restoration:

  • Torsion springs
  • Cables
  • Rollers
  • Bearings and center bearing plate
  • Drums
  • Torsion tube

If any of these fail, the replacement part is free, for as long as you own the home. That’s not a five-year lifetime or a ten-year lifetime. That’s a lifetime.

What “Lifetime” Means, Exactly

  • Parts are covered, labor is separate. After your labor warranty period ends, a warranty visit is billed at our standard service rate for the labor. The part itself costs you nothing.
  • It’s tied to you, but it can follow the house. Coverage applies to the original purchaser for as long as they own the home where we did the work. If you sell, the new owner can pick it up for a one-time transfer fee. Call us before closing and we’ll take care of it — a transferable lifetime warranty is a real thing to hand a buyer.
  • Annual maintenance keeps it active. Coverage stays in force as long as Castle performs annual maintenance on the door, currently $125 per visit. Miss a year and lifetime coverage lapses.
  • Wear items aren’t covered. Weather seals, trim, batteries, and bulbs are consumed by sun, weather, and use rather than failing from a defect. They carry no warranty on either tier.

Castle Restoration comes in two levels. Both include lifetime parts protection on the same hardware; the difference that matters here is the labor warranty — 1 year on Castle Restoration, 3 years on Restoration Premium.

Full details and pricing are on the Castle Restoration page.

If You Bought Through Home Depot or Another Retailer

Castle installs garage doors and openers as a contractor for Home Depot. When your purchase went through Home Depot, our technicians did the installation, but Home Depot is the seller — and the warranty on that purchase is administered by them, not by us.

We’re telling you this up front so you’re not bounced back and forth when you need help.

Call Home Depot, not Castle, if:

  • You paid Home Depot for the door, opener, or installation
  • Your paperwork, receipt, or financing is in Home Depot’s name
  • You have a question about what your purchase covers or how long it lasts

Home Depot will open the claim and dispatch the service — which, in most cases in our area, means us. You’ll likely see a Castle truck in your driveway either way. The difference is who authorizes the work.

Home Depot customer service: 1-800-HOME-DEPOT (1-800-466-3337), or the store where you purchased.

Call Castle directly if:

  • The door is unsafe or won’t close and you need someone out today. Call us and we’ll get it secured.
  • You want to buy something new directly from us
  • You’re not sure which category you’re in — we’ll look it up and tell you

The Same Applies to Any Third-Party Purchase

If your door, opener, or gate came through another retailer, builder, property manager, home warranty company, or a previous owner’s contractor, that seller’s terms govern the product. Castle’s 1-year parts-and-labor warranty covers work you purchased directly from Castle. It doesn’t extend to equipment someone else sold you — though we’re always happy to service it as a paid repair, and that repair will carry our normal 1-year warranty on the work we do.

What’s Not Covered

We’d rather be straight with you about this than hide it in six-point type.

  • Normal wear and tear beyond the coverage periods above
  • Cosmetic scratches, dents, or finish damage that occurs after installation
  • Rust or corrosion from coastal air, sprinklers, or standing water
  • Damage from accidents, vehicle impact, storms, flooding, earthquake, fire, lightning, or power surges
  • Damage from misuse, abuse, tampering, or forcing a door that isn’t operating correctly
  • Parts or equipment supplied by someone else
  • Failures caused by a lack of maintenance, or by pre-existing problems we identified and you declined to have repaired
  • Lifetime Restoration coverage where Castle maintenance hasn’t occurred annually
  • Wear items such as weather seals, bottom seals, trim, batteries, and light bulbs
  • Structural problems with the building — rotted jambs, settling headers, out-of-square openings — that we didn’t cause
  • Labor outside the labor warranty period on a lifetime parts claim
  • Lost income, property damage, or other consequential losses

Our warranty covers repair or replacement of the covered part. Where repair or replacement isn’t practical, we may refund what you paid us for it instead. Either way, our job is to make the system right.

If someone else works on the door

If another company adjusts, repairs, or modifies something we installed, we aren’t responsible for failures their work caused or contributed to. We also may not be able to cover a claim if their work makes it impossible for us to tell what failed and why.

That isn’t us looking for an exit. A torsion system is under enough tension to kill someone, and we can’t stand behind a setting we didn’t set.

Unrelated work doesn’t cancel your coverage. If another contractor reprograms your opener, your springs are still covered.

How we find your job

We leave a service sticker on the door or opener with your job information on it. It’s the fastest way for us to identify the work we did, but it isn’t the only way.

If the sticker is gone, damaged, or painted over, that alone doesn’t cost you your coverage. We can look up your job by name, address, or invoice number. If we can’t locate the record, we may ask you for proof of purchase or other documentation.
